# Code of Conduct

Dürbün follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work.
Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks are not accepted in issues, pull requests,
discussions or any other project space.

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer, [@mykynk](https://github.com/mykynk), privately, or
use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue. Reports are handled confidentially. The
maintainer may edit or remove contributions and block participants who do not follow this code.
